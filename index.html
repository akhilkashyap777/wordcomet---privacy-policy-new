1. Introduction

Welcome to WordComet, a platform operated by Shyaps Neon, India.

WordComet provides vocabulary learning, educational activities, professional mentorship, and online mock interview services. This Privacy Policy specifically addresses the collection, processing, storage, use, and protection of personal information associated with WordComet's interview and mentorship services.

We respect the privacy of our users, including mentors and mentees, and are committed to handling their personal information responsibly.

By using WordComet Interviews, users acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, WordComet will seek it through an appropriate consent mechanism.

2. Eligibility and Age Restrictions

WordComet Interviews is intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old.

Individuals below the age of 18 are not permitted to register for or participate in WordComet interview and mentorship services.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from an individual below the permitted age, we will take appropriate steps to restrict the account and delete the information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information

When users register or sign in, WordComet may collect:

  • Full name or display name
  • Email address
  • Google account identifier associated with Firebase Authentication
  • Account role, such as mentor or mentee

This information is used to authenticate users, manage accounts, provide access to services, and support platform operations.

3.2 Mentor Information

Mentors may provide professional information, including:

  • Professional designation or job title
  • Industry or interview domain
  • Years of professional experience
  • Résumé or curriculum vitae

This information helps WordComet manage mentor participation, evaluate professional backgrounds, and organize relevant interview opportunities.

Only limited mentor profile information is made visible to mentees. WordComet does not automatically disclose a mentor's complete professional background or uploaded résumé to other users.

3.3 Mentee Information

Mentees may provide:

  • Résumé or curriculum vitae
  • Preferred interview domain
  • Interview booking dates and times

This information is used to arrange interviews and help mentors prepare relevant questions and feedback.

3.4 Interview and Communication Information

During online interviews, WordComet may process:

  • Live camera and microphone streams
  • Screen-sharing content
  • Chat messages exchanged between participants
  • Interview feedback and ratings
  • Information necessary to facilitate interview participation

Live audio, video, and screen-sharing content are processed to deliver the interview experience.

WordComet does not intentionally record or store video interviews through its platform.

Interview chat messages are intended for communication during sessions and are not retained by WordComet after the session.

3.5 Technical and Usage Information

WordComet may collect:

  • IP addresses
  • Login timestamps
  • Application usage logs
  • Technical information required for authentication and service functionality

This information is used for troubleshooting, platform maintenance, security, and operational purposes.

4. How We Use Personal Information

WordComet processes personal information for the following purposes:

  1. Creating, authenticating, and maintaining user accounts.
  2. Managing mentor and mentee profiles.
  3. Facilitating mentor discovery and interview bookings.
  4. Enabling live video, audio, chat, and screen-sharing functionality.
  5. Allowing mentors to review résumés for booked interviews.
  6. Managing interview feedback and ratings.
  7. Providing technical support and maintaining platform security.
  8. Moderating interview sessions and investigating reported issues.
  9. Complying with applicable legal obligations.

WordComet does not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Online Interviews and Video Privacy

WordComet uses Zoom Video SDK to facilitate live online interviews.

During interviews, participants may choose whether to enable their cameras or microphones, subject to the technical requirements of the session.

Live audio, video, and screen-sharing content may be processed by the video service provider as necessary to deliver the session.

5.1 Interview Recordings

WordComet does not provide platform-managed interview recording or intentionally retain interview recordings.

Participants are permitted under WordComet's current platform rules to make their own recordings using external recording tools.

Recordings independently created by participants are not automatically collected, stored, managed, or controlled by WordComet.

Participants are responsible for complying with applicable laws, including any requirements relating to consent, privacy, confidentiality, and the use or disclosure of recorded information.

WordComet's permission to record does not remove these legal responsibilities.

5.2 Screen Sharing

Participants may voluntarily share their screens during interviews.

Information displayed during screen sharing may be visible to other participants in the session.

Users should avoid displaying passwords, financial information, confidential documents, or unrelated sensitive personal information.

5.3 Interview Chat

WordComet provides chat functionality for communication between mentors and mentees during interviews.

WordComet does not retain interview chat messages after the session.

Participants should avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily through interview chat.

6. Administrator Access and Interview Moderation

Authorized WordComet administrators may access live interview sessions for platform moderation, quality checks, technical support, and safety-related purposes.

Such access is intended to support the appropriate operation of WordComet Interviews.

Participants should be informed appropriately when authorized administrators access live sessions.

Administrator access should be restricted to authorized personnel and used only for legitimate platform purposes.

WordComet does not use administrator access to create or store routine interview recordings.

7. Résumé Collection, Storage, and Access

WordComet allows users to upload résumés for mentorship and interview-related purposes.

Uploaded résumés are stored using private Cloudflare R2 object storage.

A mentee's uploaded résumé may be accessed by:

  • The mentor associated with the relevant interview booking.
  • Authorized WordComet administrators where necessary for platform administration or support.

Mentors are permitted to use mentee résumés only to prepare for and conduct the relevant interview.

Mentors must not disclose, distribute, publish, or use mentee résumés for unrelated purposes without appropriate authorization.

Résumés may be retained while the associated account remains active, subject to valid deletion requests and applicable legal requirements.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

WordComet uses external technology providers to operate its services.

Firebase Authentication: Used for Google-based user authentication and account identification.

Cloudflare R2: Used for storing uploaded résumé documents.

Zoom Video SDK: Used to facilitate live video, audio, and screen-sharing functionality.

Hostinger / VPS Hosting: Used for hosting WordComet's backend infrastructure and associated services.

PostgreSQL: Used as the database system for storing and managing platform information.

These technologies may process information necessary to perform their respective functions.

WordComet does not authorize these services to use personal information for unrelated independent marketing on its behalf.

Data processing locations and international transfers depend on the actual configurations and practices of the relevant providers. WordComet will handle international data processing in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Storage and Security

WordComet uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information.

  • HTTPS connections for website and API communication.
  • Firebase-based authentication.
  • Role-based access controls.
  • Private résumé storage with controlled access.

Access to personal information is intended to be limited according to user roles and operational responsibilities.

Although WordComet takes measures to protect personal information, no online platform or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users are encouraged to protect their accounts and report suspected unauthorized access.

10. Data Retention

WordComet retains different categories of personal information according to their purposes.

10.1 Account Information

Account information is generally retained while the user maintains an active WordComet account, unless earlier deletion is requested or retention is otherwise required by law.

10.2 Uploaded Résumés

Uploaded résumés may be retained until the associated account is deleted or the user submits a valid deletion request, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10.3 Interview Bookings and Feedback

Interview booking records and associated feedback are intended to be retained for up to one year after the relevant interview.

Information may be retained longer where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or address legitimate security-related requirements.

10.4 Interview Media and Chat

WordComet does not intentionally store live interview recordings or retain interview chat messages after sessions.

10.5 Technical Logs

Technical and usage logs may be retained for operational, troubleshooting, and security purposes for periods appropriate to those purposes and applicable legal requirements.

WordComet uses essential cookies or browser storage technologies required for authentication, session management, and website functionality.

These technologies help maintain user sessions and enable essential platform features.

Browser settings may allow users to manage or remove stored information. Disabling essential storage may affect website functionality.

14. Sharing of Personal Information

WordComet may share limited personal information where necessary to operate the platform.

Examples include:

  • Sharing relevant booking information between a mentor and mentee.
  • Providing a booked mentor with access to a mentee's uploaded résumé.
  • Processing authentication information through Firebase.
  • Processing live interview media through Zoom Video SDK.
  • Storing information through authorized infrastructure providers.
  • Disclosing information where required by applicable law.

Users must not disclose another participant's personal information outside WordComet without appropriate authorization, unless disclosure is required by law.

WordComet does not sell users' personal information.
